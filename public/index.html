The table

At the table

Kept low on purpose: rerolling until it goes your way is the same as not rolling. A few are allowed so a bad reply from your provider is fixable.

A roleplay preset is an opinion about writing a beautiful scene, which is the wrong opinion for a game. The table brings its own — short turns, real consequences, and nothing spoken on your behalf. Your sampling and context settings are left alone. Turn this off to use whichever of your presets is active instead.

Off at the table, so what happened happened. Deleting is covered too — a line you can remove is a line you can rewrite in two steps. Turn it on if you would rather keep the pencil.

Extensions

Two halves, both optional. The page half draws and reacts; the server half rewrites the prompt going out and the reply coming back.

An extension runs with Hearth's own powers — there is no sandbox. Install one the way you would run any other program: because you trust where it came from.
